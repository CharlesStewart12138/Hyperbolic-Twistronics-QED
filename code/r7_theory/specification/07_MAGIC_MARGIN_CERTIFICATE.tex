\section{A preregistered strict-margin certificate}
\label{sec:r7-margin}

Let $H_A=H(0,p_A)$ be the frozen scalar anchor and suppose the frozen READY
anchor theorem supplies numbers $E_*$, $g_*>0$, and $\varepsilon_A$ such that
on the layer-even subspace
\begin{equation}
 \|(H_A-E_*)P_+\|\le\varepsilon_A,qquad
 0\le\varepsilon_A<g_*/6.
 \label{eq:r7-anchor-norm}
\end{equation}
Choose the resolution before testing any nearby twist so that
\begin{equation}
 \tau:=\varepsilon_A T\le\frac14,qquad
 \eta\ge2\varepsilon_A.
 \label{eq:r7-anchor-resolution}
\end{equation}
The frozen thresholds are
\begin{equation}
 s_*=\frac1{18},\quad p_*=\frac1{24},\quad r_*=\frac34,
 \quad c_*=\frac34,quad l_*=\frac34.
 \label{eq:r7-thresholds}
\end{equation}
They are derived from the analytic isolation and operator-error margins, not
from R6 plots or a posteriori tuning.

\begin{proposition}[Anchor inequality certificate]
\label{prop:r7-anchor-margin}
Under \eqref{eq:r7-anchor-norm}--\eqref{eq:r7-anchor-resolution},
\begin{align}
 S_{\rm spec}(H_A)&\le(\varepsilon_A/g_*)^2<1/36,\\
 S_{\rm prop}(H_A)&\le(\varepsilon_AT)^2/3\le1/48,\\
 R_{\rm ret}(H_A)&\ge1-\tau+\tau^2/3\ge37/48,\\
 C_{\rm layer}(H_A)&=1,\\
 L_{\rm res}(H_A;E_*)&\ge
 \eta^2/(\varepsilon_A^2+\eta^2)\ge4/5.
 \label{eq:r7-anchor-five}
\end{align}
Hence every inequality in \eqref{eq:r7-strict-magic} is strict and the
dimensionless joint margin obeys
\begin{equation}\Delta_{\rm magic}\ge1/48>0.\label{eq:r7-margin}\end{equation}
The isolated target also retains gap at least $g_*-2\varepsilon_A>2g_*/3$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
For the first-shell reference the layer-even block is exactly $E_*I$.
Equation~\eqref{eq:r7-anchor-norm} bounds the spectral variance by
$\varepsilon_A^2$ and Duhamel's formula gives
$\|e^{-itH_A}\psi_x^+-e^{-itE_*}\psi_x^+\|
\le t\varepsilon_A$.  Therefore escape probability is at most
$t^2\varepsilon_A^2$, while the return amplitude is at least
$1-t\varepsilon_A$.  Time integration gives the second and third lines.
Layer exchange commutes with the full scalar Hamiltonian, so a layer-even
state has coherence one at all times.  Finally the spectral measure of
$\psi_x^+$ is supported in $[E_*-\varepsilon_A,E_*+\varepsilon_A]$; integrating
the Poisson kernel gives the fifth line.  Weyl's inequality gives the gap
statement.
\end{proof}

The certificate is meaningful also when $\varepsilon_A=0$: choose any
$\eta,T>0$; all five inequalities improve.
