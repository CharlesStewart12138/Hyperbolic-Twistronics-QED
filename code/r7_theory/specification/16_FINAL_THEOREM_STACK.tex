\begin{theorem}[Intrinsic operational non-Bloch magic]
\label{thm:r7-principal}
For the frozen scalar hyperbolic twisted-bilayer family there is a nonempty
open parameter region $\mathcal U_{\rm magic}$ with the following properties.
\begin{enumerate}
\item The exact infinite Hamiltonian is bounded and self-adjoint.
\item At every declared finite observation radius, finite energy resolution,
and finite time horizon, the local spectral concentration, propagation
suppression, return enhancement, interlayer coherence, and broadened LDOS
satisfy the preregistered strict inequalities of
\eqref{eq:r7-strict-magic} on a nonzero twist interval.
\item Inside that interval the exact-common-cover twists form the dense null
set $\Theta_C$.  Its complement is conull and dense.
\item At every twist in the complement no exact finite common periodic cover
and no exact finite Bloch representation exists, while the five magic
inequalities hold for the same infinite operator.
\item For observation scale $\ell$ the certified angular half-width has the
explicit lower bound \eqref{eq:r7-exponential-window}, hence is positive for
every finite $\ell$ and decays as $e^{-\ell/R}$ at fixed proof budget.
\end{enumerate}
Thus the operational magic and the obstruction to finite periodicity occur at
the same conull dense set of parameter points.  They are not two statements
about different models or different angles.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Combine Theorems~\ref{thm:r7-wellposed}, \ref{thm:r7-anchor}, and
\ref{thm:r7-inheritance}, Corollary~\ref{cor:r7-any-scale}, and the frozen R5
normalizer and commensurator theorems.  The finite-resolution observables are
intrinsic by Theorems~\ref{thm:r7-ct}, \ref{thm:r7-dyn}, and
\ref{thm:r7-fixed-generic}.  R5 supplies the exact large-supercell exclusion.
\end{proof}

\label{cor:r7-paper-claim}

non-Bloch setting and preserves its finite-resolution local spectral and

\end{corollary}
