\subsection{Generic non-Bloch magic at finite resolution}

The centred hyperbolic anchor has an exactly flat layer-even first-shell
operator.  Restoring the complete exponentially decaying kernel changes that
operator by a certified norm $\varepsilon_A<g_*/6$, while preserving the
isolated Riesz sector and exact layer parity.  For a local layer-even state,
this single bound implies spectral variance at most $\varepsilon_A^2$,
finite-time escape probability at most $t^2\varepsilon_A^2$, return amplitude
at least $1-t\varepsilon_A$, exact unit layer coherence, and a broadened local
resolvent peak at least
$\eta^2/(\eta^2+\varepsilon_A^2)$.  With
$\varepsilon_AT\le1/4$ and $\eta\ge2\varepsilon_A$, these estimates satisfy a
five-component operational magic certificate with joint strict margin at
least $1/48$.

The infinite twisted operator is bounded and self-adjoint whenever the hopping
decay exponent $\mu$ exceeds the hyperbolic growth exponent $R^{-1}$.  Weighted
Schur, Combes--Thomas, and Duhamel estimates make its finite-broadening local
spectrum and finite-time dynamics exponentially local.  On a ball of radius
$L$, a centred angular perturbation obeys
\begin{equation}
 \|P_L[H(\theta)-H(\theta')]P_L\|
 \le \mu R\sinh(L/R)C_{\rm Schur}|\theta-\theta'|.
\end{equation}
Consequently the strict certificate persists on a nonzero open twist interval,
whose half-width scales as $e^{-L/R}$ for every prescribed finite observation
scale.

R5 proves that exact common-cover angles form a dense measure-zero set and
that every angle in its complement has no exact finite periodic cover.  The
intersection of that complement with the open magic interval is therefore
conull and dense.  At those same parameter points the exact infinite operator
has the five strict magic diagnostics but no finite Bloch representation.
Hence the result is not a very large exact supercell.  Negative curvature
places a rigorously certified magic mechanism in a generic non-Bloch setting
and preserves its finite-resolution local spectral and dynamical signatures.
