\section{An operational definition of non-Bloch magic}
\label{sec:r7-definition}

Fix a site $x$, the layer-even local state
\begin{equation}
 \psi_x^+=|x\rangle\otimes(|1\rangle+|2\rangle)/\sqrt2,
\end{equation}
an energy resolution $\eta>0$, a time horizon $T>0$, an observation radius
$\ell>0$, and the anchor isolation scale $g_*>0$.  Let $W_\ell$ be the
diagonal contraction
$W_\ell(y)=\min\{1,d(x,y)^2/\ell^2\}$ (with $W_\ell(x)=0$).  Define
\begin{align}
 S_{\rm spec}(H)&=g_*^{-2}
 \left(\langle H^2\rangle_{\psi_x^+}
       -\langle H\rangle_{\psi_x^+}^2\right),\\
 S_{\rm prop}(H)&=T^{-1}\int_0^T
 \langle e^{-itH}\psi_x^+,W_\ell e^{-itH}\psi_x^+\rangle\,dt,\\
 R_{\rm ret}(H)&=T^{-1}\int_0^T
 |\langle\psi_x^+,e^{-itH}\psi_x^+\rangle|^2\,dt,\\
 C_{\rm layer}(H)&=T^{-1}\int_0^T
 \langle e^{-itH}\psi_x^+,
 (I\otimes\sigma_x)e^{-itH}\psi_x^+\rangle\,dt,\\
 L_{\rm res}(H;E_*)&=-\eta\,
 \Im\langle\psi_x^+,(E_*+i\eta-H)^{-1}\psi_x^+\rangle.
 \label{eq:r7-magic-vector}
\end{align}
The dimensionless vector
\begin{equation}
 \mathcal M_{\rm NB}=(S_{\rm spec},S_{\rm prop},R_{\rm ret},
 C_{\rm layer},L_{\rm res})
\end{equation}
is the non-Bloch magic certificate.  It requires no quasimomentum, Bloch
velocity, Bloch Hessian, or finite cell.

\begin{definition}[Strict operational non-Bloch magic]
For thresholds fixed independently of the tested parameter point, $H$ is
operationally magic at resolution $(\ell,\eta,T)$ if
\begin{equation}
 S_{\rm spec}<s_*,\quad S_{\rm prop}<p_*,\quad
 R_{\rm ret}>r_*,\quad C_{\rm layer}>c_*,\quad L_{\rm res}>l_*.
 \label{eq:r7-strict-magic}
\end{equation}
It is \emph{non-Bloch magic} at angle $\theta$ if, in addition,
$\theta\notin\Theta_C$, the exact-common-cover angle set of R5.
\end{definition}

These objects are exact observables of the infinite operator.  Finite-ball
matrices are used only to approximate them with the errors proved in
Sections~\ref{sec:r7-resolvent} and~\ref{sec:r7-dynamics}.  In a periodic
model, spectral concentration and propagation suppression recover the usual
flat-band implications after Bloch decomposition, but that decomposition is
not part of the definition.
